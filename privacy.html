Privacy Policy for DashLife: Life Simulator

Effective November 4, 2026 · App ID com.fennix.lifespan

In short: your game is saved only on your device. The Android app shows optional ads from Google AdMob, which uses a device advertising identifier, and sells one optional upgrade through Google Play. We never collect your name, email or location.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect or receive any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it does not create accounts. Character names you type are fictional and stay on your device.

How your game is saved

Your lives, achievements, settings and graveyard are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to copy a backup code yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes your saves.

Backup codes

The app can show a backup code containing your saved game so you can move it to another device. The code is only created when you ask for it, and only you decide where to paste or store it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app can show a short video ad, but only when you tap a button offering a bonus in exchange for watching one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information and how you interact with the ad.

Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the in-game menu under Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Players who buy DashLife Plus are not shown ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

DashLife Plus is an optional one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns DashLife Plus so it can unlock it.

Analytics and permissions

  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • It uses internet access only to load ads and check purchases.
  • It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Third parties

We do not sell any data. The only third-party services in the app are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases and installs), each covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

DashLife is intended for players aged 18 and over because it contains simulated gambling with in-game money. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
